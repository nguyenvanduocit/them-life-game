# 01 - Genre Landscape: Life Simulator Games

Research date: 2026-10-06. Scope: definition, taxonomy, history, catalog, market signals, stickiness, conventions, white space. Single-game deep dives live in files 02-10.

Confidence labels: **HIGH** = figure read in a source I opened this session. **MEDIUM** = from a search-result snippet I did not open, or my inference from verified facts. **LOW** = guess. Where a number could not be verified it says "unverified".

## 1. Definition

- Wikipedia's definition: a life simulation game is one where "the player lives or controls one or more virtual characters (human or otherwise)", focused on "maintaining and growing a virtual life". Four subgenres are named there: digital pets, biological simulation, social simulation (virtual dollhouse, farming), raising simulation. [Wikipedia: Life simulation game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_simulation_game) - HIGH
- Working definition for this project (MEDIUM, my synthesis): a game whose primary object of play is a *life* (a person, household, creature or lineage) and whose primary pleasure is *watching, steering or role-playing that life's consequences* rather than winning a contest. The loss condition is usually death or the end of a time budget, not "game over" in the arcade sense.
- The genre label is loose. Marketplaces tag as "life sim" games that share only the fantasy of "another life": a text event generator (BitLife), a dollhouse (The Sims), a farm (Stardew Valley), an avatar chat (Zepeto). Treat it as a family of loops, not one loop. (MEDIUM, inference from the catalog below.)

## 2. Taxonomy

Axes that separate the sub-genres (MEDIUM, synthesis): (a) *time scale* (one day, one life, many generations); (b) *control* (direct control vs. choosing among events vs. observing autonomous agents); (c) *presentation* (text, 2D avatar, 3D); (d) *social* (single-player vs. other humans present); (e) *session shape* (5-minute snack vs. 100-hour sandbox).

| # | Sub-genre | Core loop | Time scale | Exemplars (verified in catalog below) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Text / event-driven life-path sim** | Age a year, read an event, pick a choice, stats move | Whole life, minutes per year | BitLife, Alter Ego, Life Restart Simulator, Reigns (ruler variant) |
| 2 | **Avatar / household sim** | Autonomous characters with needs; player steers and builds | Days to generations | The Sims 1-4, inZOI, Paralives, Sims FreePlay, Tomodachi Life |
| 3 | **Social avatar-life / UGC world** | Dress avatar, hang out with real people, buy cosmetics | Open-ended | Second Life, IMVU, Zepeto, Avakin Life, Roblox Brookhaven/Bloxburg/Adopt Me, Toca Life World |
| 4 | **Cozy farming / village life** | Daily chores, relationships with NPCs, seasonal calendar | Seasons and years | Harvest Moon, Animal Crossing, Stardew Valley, Dreamlight Valley, Hello Kitty Island Adventure, Palia |
| 5 | **Career / biography sim** | Pick path, grind skills, hit milestones | Career arc | Game Dev Story (company arc), Chinese Parents (schooling arc), Sims career tracks |
| 6 | **Raising / parenting sim** | Schedule a child's weeks; stats decide ending | Childhood to adulthood | Princess Maker, Chinese Parents, Long Live the Queen, Uma Musume (gacha-era descendant), Tokimeki Memorial (dating variant) |
| 7 | **Dynasty / legacy sim** | Manage a bloodline over generations | Centuries | Crusader Kings III, Reigns, Virtual Villagers (tribe), Sims legacy play |
| 8 | **Management hybrid** | Run an organisation as a life-flavoured sim | Open-ended | Game Dev Story, Kairosoft-style titles |
| 9 | **"Life restart" (China)** | Roll talents, auto-simulate a life, share the absurd result, restart | One life in ~5 min, replay-heavy | Life Restart Simulator and its clones/AI remakes; cultivation sims (Tale of Immortal, Amazing Cultivation Simulator) |
| 10 | **Idle / incremental life sim** | Numbers go up while away; life choices are upgrades | Persistent | Idle Life Sim (Codigames), A Usual Idle Life |
| 11 | **Digital pet / artificial life** | Feed, train, watch creature | Real-time days | Tamagotchi, Creatures, SimLife, Little Computer People |
| 12 | **AI-native NPC life sim (emerging)** | LLM-driven characters with memory | Open-ended | EmemeTown, Wanderfolk, inZOI "Smart Zois" |

## 6. What makes the genre sticky

1. **Core fantasy: "live a different life" and "what if".** The Sims began as a way to model rebuilding a life after a house fire (Berkeleyside). Life Restart's creator observed players felt "there is no optimal solution in life" and the game "hit a nerve" (Baidu Baike). HIGH for the quotes; the "what if" framing is my inference (MEDIUM).
2. **Emergent storytelling you retell.** Tomodachi Life's appeal rests on absurd Mii skits and comes with a sales run of 18.49M copies across the series. Players share outcomes (Life Restart 200M visits in 3 days is shareability proof). HIGH (numbers) / MEDIUM (causal claim).
3. **Low-effort, high-variance outcomes.** BitLife's 2018 launch was "deliberately plain, text-based" and reached #1 on iOS in three countries with zero marketing (Candywriter About). HIGH. Reigns' swipe format is 4 meters and 1 gesture. HIGH.
4. **Replayable by design.** Death or restart is a feature: Reigns (reign ends, new monarch), Life Restart (restart button), BitLife (die, start new). HIGH for each mechanic.
5. **Need-driven autonomy creates drama.** The Sims AI is built on Maslow's hierarchy of needs. HIGH (Wikipedia via search summary).
6. **Real-time companionship.** Animal Crossing uses the console clock so the village continues when you are away; Tamagotchi demanded care in real time (~98M sold). HIGH.
7. **Comfort and escapism.** NH's launch coincided with lockdowns and was used for weddings and graduations. HIGH. [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_New_Horizons)
8. **Cultural resonance.** Chinese Parents (satire of gaokao pressure) hit #2 on Steam; Princess Maker spoke to a generation of Korean women. HIGH.
9. **Cheap to produce, cheap to clone.** Life Restart was made in two weeks and spawned WeChat mini-program, AI and cloud-deployed clones on GitHub; the official mobile port suffered piracy and a 4.3-star rating. HIGH. This is a risk for us: defensibility needs more than the format.

## 7. Genre conventions and player expectations

Evidence level: conventions below are inferred from the verified titles unless a source is shown (MEDIUM overall).

- **Birth-to-death arc with stat bars** (health, happiness, smarts, looks in BitLife; four attributes in Life Restart: appearance, intelligence, physique, family background). [Baidu Baike](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Life%20Restart%20Simulator/3289950) HIGH for Life Restart.
- **Randomised starting conditions** ("talent draw" in Life Restart; random birth stats in BitLife). Conveys fate and luck. HIGH for Life Restart.
- **Yearly or turn-based event cards** with 2-4 choices and visible consequences. HIGH (Life Restart >1,500 events; BitLife description).
- **Moral freedom including crime and dark outcomes.** BitLife's store listing says choices range "from model citizenship to criminal activities" and rates 18+ with "Frequent" mature themes. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bitlife-life-simulator/id1374403536) HIGH.
- **Ending summary and a score or tag** (life score in Life Restart; reign length in Reigns). MEDIUM.
- **Free to download, ads and a one-time "remove ads" unlock.** BitLife's Bitizenship is $7.99-$9.99, God Mode $9.99, expansion packs $7.99. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bitlife-life-simulator/id1374403536) HIGH. Players complain about ad frequency and repeated events across lives (search summary of reviews; MEDIUM). Monetisation numbers belong to another file.
- **Expansion by themed packs** (Sims 4 has 21 expansion packs). HIGH.
- **Customisation of the avatar** is a baseline expectation in every avatar/social title (Zepeto fashion items sold >2.3B per Korea Herald). HIGH.
- **Cosy tone for farming/village titles, absurdist tone for text/social titles.** MEDIUM.
- **Real-world brand collabs** in social avatars: Zepeto with Dior, Gucci, Nike, Ralph Lauren. HIGH. [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/2806164)
- **Player-made content** is an expectation on PC (inZOI Canvas: 470,000+ creations on launch day; Paralives no paid DLC). HIGH.

## 8. Open gaps and white space

All items are my analysis from verified evidence (MEDIUM unless noted).

1. **Text life-path leader has a stale product against a modern cohort.** BitLife is eight years old (Candywriter About) with ad-and-paywall complaints and event repetition (review summaries). A fresh entry can win on freshness of events, UI, and fair monetisation. HIGH on age; MEDIUM on complaints.
2. **Mobile avatar-household sim is vacant.** The Sims Mobile is gone (Jan 2026) and Project Rene is unreleased and aimed at multiplayer. Mobile households with real depth have no incumbent. HIGH on the shutdown; MEDIUM on "vacant" (Sims FreePlay still runs, Tomodachi Life is Switch-only).
3. **Generational / legacy play on mobile.** Strong on PC (CK3 >4M, Sims legacy) and as swipe format (Reigns 2M), but no mainstream mobile "live a life then play your child" title was found. MEDIUM (absence of evidence).
4. **Life-restart format outside China.** Life Restart's virality (200M visits in 3 days) has no clearly dominant Western mobile polished equivalent found; clones exist as GitHub/AI remakes. MEDIUM.
5. **Raising sims in the West.** Raising sims earn enormous revenue in Japan (Uma Musume >$2B, ~90% Japan) and succeed in China (Chinese Parents), but I found no Western mobile hit in the category. MEDIUM.
6. **AI-driven NPCs are early.** inZOI, EmemeTown and Wanderfolk use LLM-driven NPCs, but inZOI's generative AI announcement drew negative reviews (Wikipedia). Execution risk, not yet a settled expectation. HIGH on backlash; LOW on others.
7. **Cosy mobile titles keep leaving mobile live-service.** Pocket Camp pivoted to paid offline; Dreamlight Valley chose premium. A fair paid or hybrid model has precedent. HIGH.
8. **Defensibility problem.** The format is trivially cloneable (Life Restart clones, piracy). Gap: content depth, brand voice, community and live content cadence as moat. HIGH on clone evidence; MEDIUM on remedy.
9. **Accessible, non-adult text life sim.** BitLife is rated 18+. A teen-safe or "life-lessons" framing is open. HIGH on the rating; MEDIUM on demand.

## 9. Implications for our mobile life-sim game

1. **Pick one sub-genre loop, not "life sim" in general.** The label spans text events, dollhouses, farming and avatar chat with unrelated loops and audiences (section 2). Decide: event-driven text life (BitLife / Life Restart lane) vs. household sim. Confidence: HIGH that the choice matters; the pick is a product decision.
2. **Shortest path to proof is the text/event lane.** BitLife reached #1 on iOS in three countries with zero marketing and a plain UI; Life Restart was built in two weeks and got 200M visits in 3 days. Prototype cost is low enough to test virality early.
3. **Design for retellable outcomes.** Every viral entry (Life Restart score, Tomodachi skits, Chinese Parents) produces a screenshot-able, shareable result. Build a shareable end-of-life summary into the core loop.
4. **Build a moat beyond the format.** Clones appeared within days (Life Restart) and piracy hit its mobile port. Plan for content volume, distinctive writing voice, localisation (e.g. Vietnamese plus English), live events, and persistent meta-progression.
5. **Treat the mobile Sims slot as the larger upside and the larger risk.** The Sims Mobile closed after ~8 years, Life by You ended in a $19.2M write-down (a write-down, not total development cost), and inZOI/Paralives show PC demand. If we target a household sim on mobile, scope it to a narrow, finishable loop.
6. **Add legacy/generational play as a differentiator.** Raising sims earn big in Asia (Uma Musume, Chinese Parents) and dynasty play is proven (Reigns, CK3), yet the West lacks a mobile hit. "Play your child next" or "pass traits across lives" turns replayability into progression.
7. **Choose a monetisation model that avoids BitLife's reported pain points.** Players cite ad frequency, ad-removal bugs, paywalled cosmetics (review summaries, MEDIUM). Keep the free experience uninterrupted at decision moments; Life Restart's "watch ads to gain talents" mobile mechanic coincided with a 4.3 TapTap rating.
8. **Decide an age-rating stance early.** BitLife is 18+ on iOS with "Frequent" mature themes; a milder rating widens reach but loses the "dark path" appeal that fuels shareable stories. Choose deliberately.
9. **Use AI sparingly and visibly as a content aid, not a headline.** inZOI's generative-AI announcement triggered negative reviews; AI life-sim clones of Life Restart are common, so "AI-generated events" is no longer a differentiator. Keep hand-authored core events with AI as optional variation.
10. **Keep a cosy/comfort option in the design space.** Cozy titles (Stardew >50M, NH >50M, Palia >10M) show durable demand; a low-stress mode or tone can broaden the audience. Mobile cozy live-service has failed twice (Pocket Camp, Sims Mobile), so validate retention before heavy live-ops investment.
