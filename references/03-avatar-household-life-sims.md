## 1. The Sims Mobile (EA / Maxis, then Firemonkeys, then Slingshot) - CLOSED

- **Launch / platform:** 2018-03-06, iOS and Android. Development moved Maxis to Firemonkeys (2019) to Slingshot (2020-2026). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sims_Mobile (HIGH)
- **Core loop:** create Sims, run careers and relationships, build and decorate. Career events (timed) and story-style relationship chains drive progress. https://help.ea.com/ca/help/the-sims/the-sims-mobile/the-sims-mobile-careers-and-career-events/ (via search summary, MEDIUM)
- **Energy gating:** every action costs Sim energy. A "power nap" restores 10 energy, once per 24 hours; Cupcakes (consumable) restore more; SimCash (hard currency) tops up. Source: search-result summary of EA/community pages, MEDIUM. EA's own farewell offered "unlimited energy" in the final weeks. https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/amp/news/the-sims-mobile-our-final-update (HIGH)
- **Timers:** events "usually either require active player involvement or an hours-long wait", skippable with SimCash (MEDIUM, search summary of pocketgamer.biz https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-does-the-sims-mobile-monetise/).
- **Currencies:** Simoleons (soft, quests, furniture/clothes) and SimCash (hard, small quest drips, IAP packs from $4.99 for 250 to $99.99 for 7,500). Same source, MEDIUM.
- **Social:** attending other players' parties, sticker ratings (Wikipedia, HIGH).
- **Reception:** Metacritic 73; The Verge: "more focused, accessible"; Shacknews: "endless barrage of microtransactions". Topped App Store at launch; $15-25M in first months. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sims_Mobile (HIGH for the quote, MEDIUM for revenue range)
- **Lifecycle:** content frozen at reruns from 2024-01-29, delisted 2025-10-21, servers off 2026-01-20; saves lived on EA servers and were deleted; no refunds for leftover currency. EA gave no reason. (HIGH)
- **Lessons:** (1) A server-side save is a liability: the player's household disappears when the service ends. (2) Energy plus hard-currency timers produced the main player complaint. (3) Seven years of content reruns did not keep the game viable; EA gave no reason, so cause is unverified.

## 2. The Sims FreePlay (EA, Firemonkeys) - LIVE

- **Launch:** iOS 2011-12-15, Android 2012-02-15. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sims_FreePlay (HIGH)
- **Time model:** real-time. "Runs in real-time, and takes real time to complete actions." Wait times of hours divide players (AppGamer, via Wikipedia). 60 levels unlock content; six life stages with aging via birthday cakes; up to 34 Sims. (HIGH)
- **Loop:** assign tasks (cook, hobby, job, shift) with real-time timers, collect Simoleons / XP / Lifestyle Points, level up, unlock buildings and items, visit friends' towns for Social Points. https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/The_Sims_FreePlay/Currency and EA forum thread (via search summary, MEDIUM)
- **Currencies:** Simoleons, Lifestyle Points (LP, scarce; skip tasks, cupcakes, rare items), Social Points (earned mostly by visiting friends). LP IAP $2.99 for 15 up to $99.99 for 3,500. VIP status from spending raises the Sim cap. (MEDIUM)
- **Live-ops:** seasonal events; Aug 2026 "Tale of Knights" season pass: 60 levels, Free and Premium tiers, premium has build/buy and CAS items and house templates. https://simscommunity.info/2026/08/14/sims-freeplay-season-pass-update/ (HIGH)
- **Reception:** Metacritic 80 (HIGH). Aug 2026 player reaction to the pass: "takes both the 'free' and 'play' out of freeplay", forced daily engagement to avoid wasting money, build-mode icons called "AI slop". (HIGH for the fact that complaints exist; individual quotes are community posts)
- **Lessons:** 15 years of life from a timer-based task loop plus VIP, but monetization creep (season pass gating templates) is now the loudest complaint. Social Points tie friends' towns to a currency, giving a reason to visit.

## 3. The Sims 4 (design reference) - LIVE, PC/console

- **Needs:** hunger, bladder, hygiene, social, fun, energy. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sims_4 (HIGH; inZOI lists 8, see section 15)
- **Emotions:** replaced the binary happy/sad of earlier Sims with 14 feelings (PC Gamer first look, MEDIUM: https://www.pcgamer.com/the-sims-4-first-look-getting-emotional-with-maxis-latest-life-sim/ ; fetch returned only a partial page). Emotions arise from moodlets, traits and events, last minutes to days, and the dominant one gates which interactions are available (search summary of Sims community guides, MEDIUM).
- **Emotion as gameplay:** producer Ryan Vaughan: "they have really deep personalities... combined that with what we're doing with their emotions, it's bringing them to life". An angry Sim builds gym skill faster; a Sim who loves cleaning is happier cleaning. Emotion drives posture, walk and animation. https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-art-of-injecting-emotion-into-i-the-sims-4- (HIGH)
- **Whims / wants:** two whims at a time from aspiration, traits and skills, plus a third whim tied to the current emotion that expires with it (search summary, MEDIUM). Fears: no source found that Sims 4 has a fears system; unverified.
- **Aspirations and traits:** long-term goals that unlock rewards; traits (e.g. "Art Lover" gives inspired moodlets) trigger random moodlets and special interactions (search summary of guides, MEDIUM).
- **Build/buy and CAS:** big pillar of the creative loop; the Sims 4 launched without pools, toddlers and Create-a-Style, which fed the "content held back for DLC" backlash. Wikipedia (HIGH).
- **Business:** launched 2014-09; free-to-play base game Oct 2022, +31M players; 85M+ by May 2024 (Wikipedia, HIGH). Steam peak 96,328 concurrent on 2022-10-23 (tracker via search summary, MEDIUM). Aug 2026: 6th in PC monthly active users and 8th in PC revenue per Newzoo as reported by simscommunity (HIGH that the article says so): https://simscommunity.info/2026/10/01/the-sims-4-pc-charts-revenue-aug-26/
- **Lessons:** the sim's depth is moodlet-stacking plus autonomy that makes behavior legible (animation, speech) and ties to player choices. Monetization is packs/kits, not timers. The emotion layer is the part to copy; the pack model is the part mobile cannot copy.

## 5. Tomodachi Life (Nintendo) - 3DS and Switch

- **3DS (2013 JP / 2014 intl):** Tomodachi Life sold 6.72M. Mii residents on an island, autonomous friendships, fights, romance and marriage; player grants food, clothes, advice; "no end condition". Intl release criticized for no same-sex relationships; Nintendo apologized. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomodachi_Life (HIGH)
- **Living the Dream (2026-04-16, Switch):** up to 70 Miis; redesigned Mii Maker with non-binary option, romantic preferences, personality "temperament" traits, face paint drawing, robotic speech with pitch and speed; Miis' needs fulfilled through minigames and problem solving to raise happiness and earn currency; Nintendo EPD with Intelligent Systems and Bandai Namco; 9 years of development from 2017; UGC system took 6-7 years. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomodachi_Life:_Living_the_Dream (HIGH)
- **Status check:** the "2026 Switch release" is verified. Wikipedia lists Switch only. A Switch 2 review exists (Console Creatures reviewed on Switch 2, 8/10), implying it runs there; a native Switch 2 edition is unverified. https://www.consolecreatures.com/tomodachi-life-living-the-dream-review/ (MEDIUM)
- **Request colors:** yellow = food/clothes, orange = friendship advice, pink = romance, purple = accidents (search summary, MEDIUM). Wishing Fountain levels the island; Palette House Workshop for custom clothes/food/rooms; "Mii News Network" for daily humor (Console Creatures, HIGH).
- **Sessions:** "at most, you're playing for an hour a day" (Console Creatures, HIGH). Another summary advises two or three 20-30 minute sessions per day, "like a Tamagotchi" (MEDIUM, source page not identified).
- **Sales and reception:** 3.8M in 2 weeks (Nintendo Life summary, MEDIUM); 8M+ by Aug 2026, best of the series (Wikipedia, HIGH; Nintendo Life headline https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/08/tomodachi-life-living-the-dream-has-already-sold-over-8-million-units). Metacritic 77 on the game page vs 78 on the series page (conflict, MEDIUM). Critics: humor and customization praised; repetition; breaking point around 50-60 hours for one reviewer; restricted image sharing and no QR/online Mii sharing; only two Miis socialize at a time (HIGH on Console Creatures; others MEDIUM).
- **Lessons:** a premium, no-timer, no-fail "watch and nudge" game sold 8M in under 5 months. The charm comes from authored absurd dialogue plus the player's own friends as characters. Weakness is long-run variety.

## 6. Zepeto (Naver Z) - avatar social platform

- **What it is:** launched 2018 by Naver's Snow; selfie to 3D avatar, then customize, social "town" spaces, photo booths, "Zepeto Studio" for creators to make and sell clothes. https://www.vice.com/en/article/zepeto-is-the-avatar-based-social-network-for-teens-thats-dominating-the-app-store/ (HIGH)
- **Currency:** virtual coins earned by IAP, daily quests (ads or waiting) and a Flappy-Bird-style minigame (Vice, HIGH).
- **Scale (stale):** 300M cumulative users, 20M MAU, 95% outside Korea, 2M+ creators, 2.3B items transacted (2022-03-04). https://www.koreaherald.com/article/2806164 (HIGH, but 2022). A later aggregator figure of 400M users and 20M MAU is MEDIUM. A "$16.4M revenue in 2026" aggregator figure is **LOW**.
- **Brand deals:** Dior, Gucci, Nike, Ralph Lauren; JYP, YG (Korea Herald, HIGH).
- **Complaints:** moderation (hate speech reported in the Vice test), privacy and ad-ID sharing (HIGH, 2018 era).
- **Lessons:** the item economy is two-sided: creators make the content, brands pay for placement. A solo household sim cannot copy that without a creator base.

## 7. Avakin Life (Lockwood Publishing)

- **Facts:** 2013 launch; 500M+ lifetime downloads; 1.6M MAU; 220K average DAU in 2025; 500M+ chats per month; 110K friendships formed per day; 47K virtual gifts per day; launched on Steam 2026-05-27. https://www.pcgamesinsider.biz/news/75555/avakin-life-surpasses-500-million-downloads-cementing-12-year-legacy-as-a-global-social-powerhouse/ (HIGH, company press numbers)
- **Read-through:** 500M downloads against 220K DAU is roughly 0.04% of downloads active daily; this ratio is my arithmetic (MEDIUM) and shows how steep install-to-daily-user drop-off is in avatar socials. Monetization is cosmetics and gifts (Wikipedia page fetch failed, so details unverified). Aggregator revenue figures ($22.3M vs $38.5M) conflict: **LOW**.
- **Lessons:** persistent social space plus gifting can sustain a 13-year product on a small active base. Child-safety posture is a core cost (not verified here).

## 8. IMVU and Second Life (brief)

- **IMVU:** revenue from virtual currency ("credits") spent on user-made items; users can become creators and earn real money. From Q4 2025 IMVU caps cumulative derivative-chain royalties at 20% of catalog price. https://support.imvu.com/support/discussions/topics/154001070852 (via search summary, MEDIUM). "4M+ MAU" is LOW (aggregator). Adweek reported $40M annual revenue in an old article: https://www.adweek.com/performance-marketing/imvu-is-generating-40-million-annually/ (HIGH that the headline exists, date old).
- **Second Life:** Linden Dollars convertible to real money, in-world land economy (search summary, MEDIUM). Paradox's Rod Humble, who led Life by You, previously led Second Life's company (cgmag calls him creator; Wikipedia calls him ex-EA Play head; treat "creator" as loose).
- **Lesson:** user-created item economies need moderation and royalty rules and are a platform bet, not a feature.

## 9. Cozy life: Hello Kitty Island Adventure, Pocket Camp, Stardew Valley (brief)

- **Hello Kitty Island Adventure (Sunblink):** Apple Arcade 2023-07-28 (Game of the Year, App Store Awards 2023), Switch/PC 2025-01-30, PS5 2025-08-05, Switch 2 2025-11-13. Loop: daily gifting to build friendship, which unlocks quests; fishing, decorating, photos; "daily limits on certain mechanics"; seasonal time-gated cosmetics. 500K+ in the first month on Switch/PC; 1M+ by Nov 2025 (Gematsu headline). Steam concurrency: all-time peak 8,326 in Feb 2025; May 2026 average ~834 (steamcharts via search summary, MEDIUM). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hello_Kitty_Island_Adventure ; https://www.gematsu.com/2025/11/hello-kitty-island-adventure-sales-top-one-million (the Gematsu snippet says "30 days" but its date is Nov 2025; sources conflict, MEDIUM).
- **Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp (Nintendo, 2017):** decorate a campsite rather than manage a town; befriend animals, complete requests for crafting materials, craft furniture via Cyrus; daily chats raise friendship; Leaf Tickets (hard currency) skip timers; "fortune cookie" gacha (Belgium restricted in Aug 2019); three subscription tiers from Nov 2019; >$250M lifetime spend by Nov 2021. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp (HIGH). Service ended 2024-11-28; replaced by Pocket Camp Complete: one-time $19.99, offline, no IAP, no multiplayer, all prior content, Leaf Tickets not transferable. https://animalcrossingpocketcamp.wiki.gg/wiki/End_of_Service (HIGH on the fact; price from Wikipedia).
- **Stardew Valley (ConcernedApe):** 50M+ sold by Feb 2026; iOS Oct 2018 grossed $1M in 3 weeks. https://www.vgchartz.com/article/467162/stardew-valley-sales-top-50-million-units/ ; https://sensortower.com/blog/stardew-valley-revenue-1-million (MEDIUM, search summaries).
- **Lesson:** "Cozy" works best with a finite daily to-do list that the player can finish in 10-20 minutes and with premium pricing; Nintendo's own mobile life sim ended on the flat-fee model.

## 10. Pocket Love (HyperBeard)

- **Facts:** couple creator, decorate multiple rooms (kitchen, bedroom, garden), adopt pets, dates (park, restaurants, K-pop concerts), minigames (pancakes, box delivery). 4.7 rating from 249K (iOS), age 9+; IAP $0.99-$9.99 for "Dogllars" and cosmetics, rewarded ads. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pocket-love/id1575412509 (HIGH). 31M downloads (search summary, MEDIUM).
- **Complaints (App Store feedback summary):** repetitive loops; wants more pet types, multiplayer/co-op, more rooms, more dialogue variety, children in-game. (MEDIUM; the summary is the fetch tool's synthesis of review text.)
- **Lessons:** a tiny two-person household with strong cute art got 31M installs; players ask for depth: family growth, social, variety.

## 11. Love Nikki and dress-up (brief)

- **Love Nikki (Elex / Papergames, 2017):** an "RPG disguised as a fashion game": collect clothes with style tags and 1-6 heart rarity, dress for themed missions, craft/evolve clothes, compete in PvP Arena. Revenue drivers by rank: stamina purchases, Princess-mission attempts, Arena attempts, gacha. "Every time they release a time limited event - they double their revenues." >$140M lifetime by 2018 analysis. https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2018/4/9/why-girls-are-ready-for-hardcore-game-systems (HIGH, 2018 data)
- **Monetization (later):** multi-tier "Hell Events" gachas costing 10,000+ diamonds ($100+); permanent VIP tiers with extra stamina cap. https://otome.com/2025/12/05/love-nikki-market-and-revenue-analysis-2025/ (MEDIUM, search summary)
- **Nikki franchise revenue, first year on mobile:** Shining Nikki $103.2M, Infinity Nikki $69.9M, Love Nikki $49.7M (pocketgamer.biz via search summary, MEDIUM). Infinity Nikki: launched 2024-12-05, F2P open-world dress-up with gacha and pity, 10M downloads in 4 days (Wikipedia, HIGH): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinity_Nikki
- **Love and Deepspace (adjacent, otome gacha):** about $749M IAP in ~2 years; limited-time event packs ~60% of revenue (playfulbraindemand.com, MEDIUM; AppMagic cites ~$1B user spend: https://appmagic.rocks/research/love-and-deepspace-monetization, not fetched).
- **Lessons:** the highest-grossing avatar-centric mobile titles in this slice are gacha + stamina + event driven; avatar customization is the product, and live events are the revenue engine.

## 15. inZOI (Krafton) - PC

- **Facts:** Steam early access 2025-03-28 (Steam page shows 2025-03-27; timezone), macOS Aug 2025, PS5 planned early 2027; Unreal Engine 5. 8 needs: hunger, hygiene, bathroom, fun, social interaction, energy, sleep, recognition. "City edit" mode, 250+ appearance options, on-device generative AI for textures and patterns, 3D object generation from 2D images, mocap for video, "Canvas" community sharing, official mod plugins. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InZOI ; https://store.steampowered.com/app/2456740/inZOI/ (HIGH)

## 16. Life by You (Paradox Tectonic) - CANCELLED

- **Timeline:** announced 2023-03-06; early access dates slid 2023-09-12, 2024-03-05, 2024-06-04; cancelled 2024-06-17; studio (24 jobs) closed the next day; write-down 208 MSEK (~$19.2M). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_by_You ; https://whatsinkedinmymind.substack.com/p/paradox-interactive-closes-down-life-by-you (HIGH)
- **Stated reason:** Paradox CEO Fredrik Wester: "a version that we'd be satisfied with is too far away"; "We've performed poorly in recent releases." https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/media/press-releases/press-release/paradox-interactive-cancels-the-release-of-life-by-you-which-impacts-the-second-quarter-with-msek-208-in-a-write-down (HIGH)
- **Dissent:** game designer Willem Delventhal on LinkedIn said the team exceeded internal metrics and staff learned from the public announcement (via substack, MEDIUM: a single person's claim).
- **Product plan:** deeply moddable; led by Rod Humble; player-choice outcomes, fine object detail (clothes on the floor, flowers in vases). Delays were spent on animation, character models, lighting, UI overhaul, mod tools, bug fixes (search summary, MEDIUM).
- **Lessons (analysis, MEDIUM):** a Sims-class bar for animation, UI, autonomy and polish was the problem; modding does not substitute for a polished base game. Scope had to ship earlier as a smaller early-access product. Contrast: Paralives sold 1M on EA with a rough but promising base and a Patreon community first.

## 17. Paralives (Paralives Studio) - PC

- **Facts:** indie developed by Alex Massé from Jan 2019; 14 staff by June 2025; Patreon with 15K+ supporters, $35K+/month (Nov 2023); Unity; "no paid DLC" with free updates; early access 2026-05-25 at $39.99; 250K copies in 8 hours; 1M in first month; Steam "Very Positive". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paralives (HIGH)
- **Early access state:** grid-free building with curved walls, character creator with body sliders, open-world town (Melino) with careers, social interactions and events, basic household management; missing pets, seasonal weather, pools/basements, cars/boats, family trees, calendar. PC Gamer: "buggy, far from feature-complete" and "a UI that is messy and oftentimes unintuitive"; 78K peak concurrent. https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317163/20260525/paralives-early-access-launches-steam-78k-players-mixed-warnings-critics.htm (HIGH)
- **Retention warning:** simscommunity (Newzoo-based article) says Paralives players dropped "approximately 97%" from launch peaks by Aug 2026. https://simscommunity.info/2026/10/01/the-sims-4-pc-charts-revenue-aug-26/ (MEDIUM; second-hand)
- **Lessons:** a sandbox build tool sells on day one; a missing household loop causes a fast fall-off. Original Dec 8, 2025 target was delayed to improve stability (search summary, MEDIUM).

## 19. Extracted design primitives

Source evidence is in the sections above. The synthesis is analysis (MEDIUM).

1. **Needs systems.** Sims 4: 6 needs. inZOI: 8 needs including "recognition". Tomodachi replaces needs with colored requests (food, clothes, advice, romance, accidents) that cost one tap each and are generated by the game. Mobile Sims use energy as the player's budget, not the Sim's. Tomodachi's request model is the cheapest form of "needs" for a phone.
2. **Time model.** Three distinct patterns: (a) real-time timers with hard-currency skips (FreePlay, Pocket Camp, Sims Mobile events); (b) daily check-in with a finite list and no fail state (Tomodachi, HKIA daily gifts); (c) offline accrual/idle (Cats & Soup, Virtual Families). Pattern (a) is the source of the loudest complaints; pattern (b) produced the 2026 hit.
3. **Progression.** Level-gated unlock trees (FreePlay 60 levels), friendship levels (HKIA, Pocket Camp), island/town levels (Tomodachi Wishing Fountain), collection and evolution (Love Nikki).
4. **Social graph.** Autonomous NPC-to-NPC relationships (Tomodachi, Sims), player-to-player visits (FreePlay Social Points, Sims Mobile parties), full social spaces (Zepeto, Avakin, IMVU). Project Rene bets on friend co-play.
5. **Customization.** The avatar is the product in Zepeto, Avakin, Love Nikki, Pocket Love. Tomodachi's UGC system took 6-7 years to build, which shows the cost of doing it well.
6. **Home building.** Core to Sims, Paralives, Pocket Love, Pocket Camp. Cheap version: fixed rooms with slot-based decoration (Pocket Love). Expensive version: free-form building (Paralives, inZOI).
7. **Aspirations.** Sims 4 aspirations plus whims give long-term direction with short-term suggestions. Mobile equivalents: career events, story chains (Sims Mobile).
8. **Personality systems.** Sims 4 traits feed emotions and interactions; Tomodachi temperament traits drive dialogue and compatibility. Traits that change visible behavior sell the "alive" feeling.
9. **Energy / stamina gating.** Sims Mobile energy, Love Nikki stamina and attempt caps (top revenue driver per Deconstructor of Fun). Plus the VIP stamina cap raise in Love Nikki.
10. **Event / quest pacing.** Time-limited events double revenue in Love Nikki; Sims Mobile and FreePlay run themed events and passes; HKIA uses seasonal cosmetics; Love and Deepspace derives ~60% of revenue from event packs.
11. **Live-ops calendars.** Observed: seasonal passes (FreePlay, Aug 2026, 60 levels), time-limited gachas (Nikki), seasonal items (HKIA). Release cadences and calendar lengths for each are unverified.

## 22. Implications for our mobile life-sim game

Each bullet is analysis (MEDIUM) anchored in the sections above.

1. **Default to a daily-check-in loop, not a timer wall.** Tomodachi (8M+ in 4.5 months, premium) and HKIA show that a finite daily list wins attention; FreePlay's timers and season pass attract the loudest complaints (sections 2, 5, 9, 21).
2. **Make "needs" a generated request, not a bar to babysit.** Tomodachi's color-coded requests are cheap to build, readable on a phone and one-tap to resolve. Keep an inZOI/Sims-style emotion layer only where it changes visible behavior (section 3, 5).
3. **Put the emotional AI budget into legible autonomy.** Sims 4's emotions work because they change animation, speech and available interactions; inZOI's autonomy and Life by You's polish gap show the penalty of shipping a sim that feels lifeless (sections 3, 15, 16).
4. **Choose a business model deliberately and early.** Evidence points two ways: premium (Tomodachi, HKIA, Paralives, Pocket Camp Complete at $19.99) vs F2P with energy and events (Nikki, FreePlay). Do not mix a premium feel with a late-added season pass (section 2 reaction).
5. **Local-first save with export.** Sims Mobile servers deleted all saves; Pocket Camp needed an offline replacement. Design the household as a portable file even if the account syncs (sections 1, 9).
6. **Cheap home building: slot-based rooms with a rich catalog.** Pocket Love reached 31M downloads with fixed rooms; free-form building (Paralives, inZOI) costs years and ends in "EA bugginess" (sections 10, 15, 17).
7. **Solve the depth cliff.** Repetition is the top complaint in Tomodachi and Pocket Love; plan generational play (Virtual Families), aspirations and authored events at design time so that day 30 differs from day 3 (sections 5, 10, 12).
8. **Social without a moderation bill.** Zepeto, Avakin and IMVU carry safety and creator-economy overhead. Prefer asynchronous friend visits or a "friend as resident" model like Tomodachi/FreePlay Social Points; Project Rene suggests EA's next step is co-op, but its design is still unreleased (sections 4, 6-8).
9. **Customization is the revenue engine in avatar titles.** If we want revenue without energy gating, price cosmetics and avatar items; Love Nikki shows events with exclusive items double revenue, but its gacha and stamina model gets harsh player reaction (section 11).
10. **Live-ops calendar from day one, kept lighter than the Nikki model.** Seasonal cosmetics (HKIA) and one weekly or monthly goal are enough; design the content pipeline before launch (sections 9, 11, 19).
11. **Use the 2026 gap.** Sims Mobile is gone, Project Rene is multiplayer, Paralives and inZOI are PC-only: there is currently no named, live, polished, single-player household sim on phones from a major publisher (analysis; it rests on my not finding one in this research, which is not proof, so verify with a store search before relying on it).
12. **Prototype order.** First prove the 10-minute daily session (requests + a few reactions + one decoration), then add one aspiration chain and a personality-to-emotion rule set. Test whether players come back on day 7 before building home construction or social.
