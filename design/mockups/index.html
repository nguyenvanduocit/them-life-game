All 28 screens of the first release: first launch, quick mode, long mode, social, meta and system states. Design probe: throwaway mockups with the story's real copy, not product code.

Each screen's background is the color of a carbon copy sheet, and it tells you what kind of screen you are on.

First launch

Age gate, AI consent, then straight into a rolled case. No tutorial and no account before the first laugh.

Quick mode

Home to a finished life in about four minutes. Every outcome gets a red stamp. The autopsy report is the share card.

Long mode: your Subject

One character who keeps living online. You decide the big moments within a window, then Jev decides. No neglect deaths, no timers on care.

Colleagues

Friends are the people in the next cubicle. Their Subjects cross yours in memos. Safety screens are plain and serious, with no jokes.

Meta: collection, rank, story, shop, settings

The reasons to come back after the first life, and the controls that make the game safe to hand to anyone.

System states

Push notifications and the outage fallback. Push text is lock-screen safe: the joke lives in the app.
