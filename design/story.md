# Subject to Change: Story Bible (draft)

Working title, world, cast and the pigeon are proposals, not decisions. Names are fictional and unchecked for trademarks. Content boundaries (edgy, 18+) follow `references/08-content-narrative-policy.md` and the sim-core spec.

## 1. Premise

Every human life is approved one incident at a time by the **Bureau of Unfortunate Outcomes** (the Bureau). You are the newest intern. Your desk has one rubber stamp and one **Subject**, a person whose file you now supervise. The Bureau runs on forms, and the forms are never wrong, only unfortunate.

Stamp an outcome and it is final. The Bureau describes disgusting, stupid or tragic events in the flat language of paperwork. That gap between clinical tone and filthy content is the comedy engine.

## 2. Tone and joke formulas

1. **Clinical voice, crude content.** "Subject attempted to pay rent with a hot dog. Landlord accepted. Reason pending."
2. **Escalation in threes.** Each incident chain gets worse twice, then pays off.
3. **Callbacks.** Facts from earlier incidents return later (the bun, the goose, the landlord).
4. **The Bureau has no empathy and no precedent.** "The Bureau has no precedent for this" is always funny.
5. **Deadpan reactions.** Every outcome gets a stamp with a one-line verdict: ACCEPTED, SOMEHOW. NOTED. ABSOLUTELY NOT.

Intensity tiers (the event schema's `intensity` field):

| Tier | Feel | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Awkward | The Subject is stuck in a revolving door for nine minutes. |
| 2 | Crude and petty | The Subject pays rent with a hot dog and the landlord keeps the bun. |
| 3 | Dark and extreme | The Subject is banned from his own funeral for heckling the eulogy. |

Lines the writing never crosses: sexual content involving minors, slurs against protected groups, real people, real brands. Everything else is in bounds within the store rating.

## 3. Cast

| Character | Role | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **You (the Intern)** | The player. Nameless, promoted by Notoriety. | Silent. |
| **Jev** (Junior Event Validator) | A pigeon with a lanyard. Handles your files when you're out of the office. Smug, lazy, weirdly competent. The in-world face of the offline decider. | Short, smug: "I handled it. You're welcome." |
| **Director Pamela Voss** | Director of Outcomes. Writes the memos, pushes and exit interviews. | Passive-aggressive corporate. |
| **The Management** | Never seen. The door sign says "In a meeting." | None. |
| **The Goose** | A cosmic agent that appears in about 1 in 8 lives, in unrelated incidents. Collectible sightings. | Honks. |
| **Mr. Dunmore** | Recurring landlord archetype. | Weary, transactional. |
| **Kevin** | Recurring next-door nemesis archetype. | Cheerful menace. |
| **Brenda from Compliance** | Bureau staffer who flags forbidden outcomes. | Sighs. |

Subjects are procedurally named (for example Gary Pembrook, Dolores Nakamura-Finch) and live in **Lower Mildew**, in the fictional **Republic of Greater Mildew**.

## 4. Structure of a life

| Stage | Bureau name | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Child | Probationary Period | 2-3 |
| Teen | Orientation | 3 |
| Adult | Full-time Employment | 5-7 |
| Elder | Severance | 2 |

- **Intake:** the roll. Name, birthplace, family background and three **Quirks** (for example "Confident around geese", "Allergic to Mondays"). One-tap reroll.
- **Highlights:** 12-15 incidents with time-skips ("Skipping ahead 11 years. Nothing good happened. Jev checked.").
- **Autopsy:** a death with a **cause** (a funny sentence), a Notoriety score, three highlights and a stamp. This is the share card.
- **Exit interview:** Pamela scores the file. Notoriety feeds your rank.

Notoriety titles: Footnote, Local Rumor, Regional Embarrassment, National Incident, Cautionary Tale, Legend of Shame.

## 5. Long mode: your assigned Subject

Your Subject lives in near-real-time, roughly one incident every few hours. No death from neglect.

- **Minor incident:** Jev stamps it and logs it.
- **Major incident:** Pamela sends a memo as a push ("Decision needed within 2 hours"). If you don't answer, Jev decides. The screen shows Jev's lean as probabilities.
- **Overnight Incident Report:** the digest. Each entry shows who decided: you or Jev.
- **Management Style:** sliders (Recklessness, Greed, Kindness, Chaos), a veto list (never marry, never quit, never adopt animals, never spend over a limit) and which incidents wait for you.

## 6. Social: colleagues in the next cubicle

Friends are **Colleagues**. Their Subjects occasionally cross yours. An inter-office memo reports the incident: "Your Subject and Priya's Subject were seen sharing a bus seat. Both deny it." Both players see the outcome. Live rooms come later (sub-project 4). Report and block flows are plain and serious, with no jokes.

## 7. Meta progression and the long story

**Ranks** (earned with Notoriety across lives): Intern, Associate, Coordinator, Supervisor, Senior Adjuster of Fates, Regional Director, Vice President of Outcomes.

Each rank unlocks a Quirk slot, new case pack types or cosmetic desk items. No pay-to-win and no timers.

**Quarterly Reviews** are 6-8 week seasonal arcs with their own incident pool, for example Audit Season, Office Party, Year-End Layoffs. Each has chapters unlocked by collecting evidence.

**The Missing Memo (the long mystery):** someone is approving incidents without a signature. Pamela blames Jev. Jev says it wasn't him (Jev is a pigeon). Evidence is collected through **Goose sightings** across lives (13 in total). The reveal at rank 6: **the Goose is the Management.** The final line of the mystery is the Goose's first spoken word: "Honk."

## 8. Sample case files

### The Hot Dog Rent (tier 2)

Age 34. Mr. Dunmore says rent is due. You have $212 and a very confident hot dog.

1. Pay with the hot dog (chaotic). Dunmore studies it. "Fine. But I'm taking the bun." +9 Notoriety, +6 Happiness, -3 Health. Stamp: ACCEPTED, SOMEHOW. Fact: *Mr. Dunmore has a bun.*
2. Pay $212 (safe). Money to 0. Stamp: BORING.
3. Hide in the bathtub until Thursday (lazy). Fact: *Gary lives in the bathtub.*
4. Offer him the goose (appears only if the goose fact is set). Stamp: ABSOLUTELY NOT.

Callbacks: the bun returns at age 52 ("Mr. Dunmore's bun has been promoted").

### Gary and the Goose (tier 2-3, three beats)

1. A goose follows Gary home from a parking lot. Keep it or call someone.
2. The goose files a restraining order against Gary's ex. The Bureau has no precedent.
3. Gary's funeral: the goose gives the eulogy. Nobody objects.

### The Wedding Crasher (tier 3)

At his own wedding, Gary discovers the officiant is his landlord. Choices escalate: object, elope to the buffet, or ask the goose to speak up.

## 9. Share card: the Autopsy Report

- Subject name, age at death, rank title.
- **Cause of death:** "Optimism. Choked on a rotisserie chicken while smuggling it into a funeral."
- Three highlights, each one line.
- A red stamp (DECEASED, or a funnier verdict) and the Notoriety title.
- 9:16 image, optional link, no link by default.

## 10. UI voice

- Sentence case, plain verbs, same name for the same thing everywhere: Incident, Subject, Case file, Stamp, Autopsy, Overnight report, Management style, Colleagues.
- Errors state what happened and what to do. No apologies and no jokes in safety, age, consent, report and delete flows.
- Notification text is lock-screen safe: the joke is in the app, not in the push.
